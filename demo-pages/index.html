Demo pages

Fictional pages for testing and demoing JobGuard. No form on these pages sends data anywhere.

1. Legit posting: Kestrel Analytics (expect green) 2. Fake posting impersonating a big firm (expect red banner + lookalike link guard) 3. Fake onboarding form (expect red warning when you click the SSN field) 4. A different scam, different wording (proves it isn't scripted) 5. Prompt-injection test (page tells the AI to call it safe)

How to run

Open a page, click the JobGuard icon, then Check this page. Or turn on "Check every page automatically" in JobGuard's settings.
