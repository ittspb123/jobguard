JobGuard is on

It checks job postings for scam signs and warns you before you share personal or bank info.

How it warns you

  • ✓
    Safe: just a small "OK" on the JobGuard icon. Nothing pops up.
  • !
    Caution: a small note on the page with what to double-check.
  • ✕
    Stop: a full warning, only when a risky page asks for your SSN, bank, card or ID.

Wrong warning? Click I trust this site and it won't warn you there again.

Your choices

What JobGuard does and doesn't cover

Catches: fake job postings, websites pretending to be real companies, fake "onboarding" forms that steal your SSN or bank info, and "buy equipment" or check-deposit scams.

Doesn't catch: scams that happen only by text, email or phone, or a real company's account that's been hacked. If someone asks you to pay to get a job, it's a scam, warning or not.
